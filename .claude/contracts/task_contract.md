# Task contract

For substantial work, establish the requested outcome, constraints and evidence
that would demonstrate success. Preserve the distinction between explicit
requirements and assumptions. Resolve routine uncertainty from the repository;
ask only when the unresolved choice changes authority or the intended result.

Use [verify-loop](../commands/verify-loop.md) for the operational procedure.
Select checks for changed behavior and plausible failures; do not manufacture a
fixed number of risks, tables or persistent logs. For research, also use
[quant-loop](../commands/quant-loop.md) and the
[promotion gate](promotion_gate.md) when assessing a verdict.

Before completion, inspect the diff and connect material claims to executed
checks, source evidence or resulting artifacts. Distinguish missing evidence,
pre-existing failures and known limitations. A test plan or successful syntax
check is not evidence that behavior was tested. The final answer should contain
what changed, why it works and what remains uncertain, in the form useful to
the user. A table is optional, not proof by itself.
