# Duplicate Resolution

Records of cases where two source files corresponded to the same paper, and how the resolution was made.

## 1. Chi et al. (1994), "Eliciting Self-Explanations Improves Understanding"

This paper has two DOIs in circulation:
- `10.1207/s15516709cog1803_3` (the *Cognitive Science* journal DOI)
- `10.1016/0364-0213(94)90016-7` (the Elsevier-mirrored DOI for the same Cognitive Science volume)

A single source file was located in `~/Downloads/`: `0364-0213%2894%2990016-7.pdf` (the Elsevier mirror). This was renamed to the canonical filename:

`learning_research/papers/06_self_explanation_and_elaboration/1994__chi__eliciting_self_explanations__self_explanation.pdf`

Both DOIs are recorded in `doi_mapping.md`. Treat them as one paper.
