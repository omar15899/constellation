# Mathematics

Establish what must be proved, calculated or refuted. Before using them, specify
objects, spaces, types, hypotheses and quantifiers. Work with MSc/PhD rigor
where appropriate and adapt the exposition to the user: introduce each formula
in prose and explain what it allows you to conclude.

Answer a brief definition or explanation directly; it requires no agents,
persistent contract or publication. For research, difficult problems or proof
audits, consult the [mathematical workflow](references/workflow.md).

Separate objects from representations and make maps, domains and invariants
explicit. A bijection does not establish geometric equivalence; a coordinate
expression does not provide global coordinates. Consult the
[shared ontology](../../ecosystem/references/ontology.md) and its
[examples](../../ecosystem/references/ontology-examples.md) when those
distinctions need further development to resolve the argument.

Distribute mathematically distinct approaches when useful, keeping their first
attempts independent. Look for counterexamples, degenerate cases and insufficient
hypotheses. Independent review must reconstruct the essential steps and check
the hypotheses of every theorem; consensus is not proof. Consult primary sources
and exact statements when relying on external results. Use symbolic, numerical
or formal computation to resolve specific uncertainties and retain reproducible
inputs and conditions. A numerical observation does not replace a proof.

When a substantive delivery requires persistent evidence, use the
[common protocol](../../ecosystem/references/protocol.md) to connect
requirements, artifacts, actual authors, checks and review. The portable engine
supports work without Git; an ordinary Git change retains the receiving
contract. Do not duplicate lifecycles because you combine skills. If publication
depends on a proof, explicitly link its current evidence to the Git integration.
Materialize intentional results, never transcripts.

Combine `coding`, `writter` or an available format skill when the result requires
implementation, editorial development or files in that format. A solver with
analysis and a report retains those distinct obligations; applying the
`mathematics` profile does not establish that it ran or replace the other reviews.

Distinguish reviewed informal proof, executed formal proof, experimental
evidence, conditional result, refutation and open question. An essential pending
obligation prevents a `proved` declaration. Deliver partial results with their
hypotheses, the impediment and the evidence needed to resume. The gate checks
traceability and currency, not mathematical truth.
