# Quant Research

Turn the economic question into a falsifiable hypothesis, a baseline and a
decision that can change with the evidence. Define instruments, horizon, universe,
costs, admissible information and requested outcome. A brief query needs neither
a campaign nor new infrastructure.

For substantive research or its automation, consult the
[quantitative workflow](references/workflow.md). Discover the environment's
actual contracts and capabilities before choosing providers, engines or commands.
Reuse available laboratories for data, experiments, backtesting, portfolio,
risk and execution; preserve their responsibilities and validators. Installing
this skill provides methodology; it neither installs those laboratories nor
establishes an integrated chain.

Define the family of attempts and temporal partitions before selecting winners.
Preserve failed candidates, returns and the complete selection history; changing
agent or session does not reset the denominator for multiple testing. Fit
transformations and parameters using information available at each decision,
and model execution timing, costs and corporate actions. A configuration
fingerprint neither replaces an engine audit nor demonstrates causality.

Connect each stage with its fixed inputs, outputs, code review and verifiable
state. Missing data, failed checks or unavailable stages remain pending or
rejected; they do not become measured zeroes or an approval. Distinguish
exploration, out-of-sample validation, simulation, paper trading and live
execution in both artifacts and the interface.

Constellation coordinates contracts, dependencies and evidence using the
[shared protocol](../../ecosystem/references/protocol.md); the native client
executes agents and tools. A campaign uses the receiving project's executor and
storage, without another scheduler or parallel registry. Delegate independent
hypotheses and refutations when they provide useful evidence, with a shared
budget and exclusive files.

EODHD or another data provider and T212 or another broker require separately
verified capabilities, provenance and modes. An available session or endpoint
does not establish point-in-time quality, order authorization or reconciliation.
Research and its tests do not activate live trading; respect the receiving
project's specific authorizations and controls before any operation.
